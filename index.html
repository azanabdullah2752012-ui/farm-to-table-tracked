Standardized Core Architecture

The 4 Compulsory Tracking Points

Mandatory data fields embedded on every thermal crate QR sticker and decoded on the consumer's phone.

01

Batch ID

Unique cryptographic identification code stamped at the farm gate so every vegetable crate can be traced individually through every transport leg.

Example: #TM-8402-NSK
02

Quantity

Exact weight in kilograms (KG) and standardized crate count, auditing transit moisture evaporation and eliminating middleman pilferage.

Example: 500.0 KG (25 Crates)
03

Harvest Place & Date

True GPS farm village coordinates and verified harvest timestamp. Middlemen can never backdate or falsify old produce as "morning fresh".

Example: Dindori, Nashik • 06:15 IST
04

Freshness Level

Dynamic freshness percentage (%) and official grade (Grade A+), updated continuously by transit temperature logs and time elapsed.

Example: 94% (Grade A+ Safe)

How Farm to Table Tracked Works

A ₹0.02 paper tag at harvest eliminates thousands of crores in food spoilage down the road.

1️⃣

Farm Gate Crate Tagging

The farmer opens the Farmer App on any low-end smartphone, logs crop, quantity, and place, and prints a durable thermal QR sticker for ₹0.02.

2️⃣

Cold-Chain Temperature Sync

During truck transit, reusable Bluetooth BLE sensor pucks log crate temperature every 15 minutes, guaranteeing perishable greens stay below 6°C.

3️⃣

Shopper Camera Scan

The consumer opens the Shopper App at the mandi or supermarket, scans the crate QR, and sees the authentic Freshness Passport.
